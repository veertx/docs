# For Receivers

## Setup (one time)
1. Go to veertx.com/setup
2. Connect your Solana wallet (Phantom, Solflare, Backpack, or any WalletConnect wallet)
3. Sign a message. This generates your stealth keys locally in your browser. Your private keys never leave your device.
4. Choose a username
5. Your VeerLink is ready: veertx.com/pay/yourname

Share your VeerLink publicly. Your actual wallet address is never revealed.

## Claiming
1. Go to veertx.com/claim
2. Connect the same wallet used during setup
3. Sign to prove ownership
4. Sweep funds to any wallet you choose

## Telegram Notifications
Optional. Connect your Telegram to receive private alerts at each stage:
- When funds arrive at your deposit address
- When anonymization starts
- When funds are ready to claim
