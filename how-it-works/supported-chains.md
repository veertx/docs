# Supported chains

VeerTx is live on two chains today. You choose the chain when you create a payment.

## Solana

| Token | Status |
|-------|--------|
| SOL   | Live   |
| USDC  | Live   |

## Base

| Token | Status |
|-------|--------|
| ETH   | Live   |
| USDC  | Live   |

## Ethereum

Ethereum (the main network) is planned for later. It is not live yet.

## How the chain is chosen

The receiver can link more than one chain to the same account. On the pay page, the sender sees the chains the receiver has linked.

- Chains the receiver has linked show as ready to pay.
- Chains they have not linked are shown but cannot be selected yet.

[SCREENSHOT: pay page chain selector showing Solana and Base]

## A note on USDC

USDC works on both Solana and Base. They are different tokens on different networks. Make sure you send the right one on the right chain. The pay page always shows you exactly which chain and token to use.
