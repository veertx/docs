# Receive payments

This guide shows you how to set up your VeerTx link so people can pay you privately. You only do this once.

## Before you start

You need a crypto wallet. On Solana you can use Phantom, Solflare, Backpack, or any wallet that supports WalletConnect. On Base you can use a compatible Ethereum wallet.

You do not need any funds in your wallet to set up a link. You can claim payments later even with an empty wallet.

## Steps

### 1. Open the setup page

Go to:

```
veertx.com/setup
```

[SCREENSHOT: setup page first screen with the username field and chain choice]

### 2. Pick a username and chain

Choose a username. This becomes part of your link. Pick the chain you want to receive on (Solana or Base).

Your link will look like this:

```
veertx.com/pay/yourname
```

### 3. Connect your wallet

Click continue and connect your wallet. Approve the connection in your wallet.

### 4. Sign one message

Your wallet asks you to sign a message. This is free. It does not move any funds.

This signature creates your private payment keys inside your browser. The keys never leave your device, and VeerTx never sees them.

[SCREENSHOT: wallet popup asking to sign the setup message]

### 5. Your link is ready

That is it. Your link is now live. Copy it and share it anywhere.

[SCREENSHOT: success screen showing the finished pay link and a copy button]

## Adding a second chain

You can link more than one chain to the same account. After setup, open your link or the setup page again, connect, and add the other chain. Both chains then work under the same username.

## What happens next

When someone pays your link, the funds are made private and then become claimable by you. See [Claim your funds](claim-your-funds.md).

To get alerts when money arrives, see [Telegram alerts](telegram-alerts.md).

## Good to know

- Your wallet address is never shown on your pay page.
- You can send claimed funds to any wallet you choose. Your ability to claim is tied to your setup signature, not to a wallet balance.
- Keep access to the wallet you set up with. You need it to claim.
