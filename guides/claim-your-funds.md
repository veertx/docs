# Claim your funds

When someone pays your link, the funds are made private and then become ready to claim. This guide shows you how to claim.

## When can you claim

After a payment is sent, it takes about 10 to 45 minutes to pass through the privacy pool. After that, it shows up on your claim page as ready.

If you set up Telegram alerts, you get a message when funds are ready. See [Telegram alerts](telegram-alerts.md).

## Steps

### 1. Open the claim page

Go to:

```
veertx.com/claim
```

### 2. Connect your wallet

Connect the same wallet you used at setup. This is how VeerTx knows the payments are yours.

[SCREENSHOT: claim page asking to connect a wallet]

### 3. Sign to prove ownership

Your wallet asks you to sign a message. This is free and moves no funds on its own. It proves you own the account.

[SCREENSHOT: wallet popup asking to sign for claim]

### 4. See your payments

You see a list of payments ready to claim. Each one shows the amount and the chain. If a sender left a private message, you can read it here.

[SCREENSHOT: claim page list with one or more payments and a Claim button]

### 5. Claim

Click claim on a payment. Approve in your wallet. The funds are swept to your wallet.

You can claim to any wallet you control. Your funds are yours to move.

## How long do you have

Payments stay claimable for 7 days after they are ready. Claim within that window.

## Notes

- A wallet with no funds can still claim. You do not need to pay gas out of an empty wallet for a standard claim.
- Each claim needs one signature. That is normal and expected.
- If a claim seems stuck, leave it a few minutes and refresh. The page keeps checking in the background.

## Private messages

If a sender attached a private message, it shows next to the payment. The message is removed 24 hours after you claim. VeerTx never sends message content in Telegram alerts.
