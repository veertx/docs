# Telegram alerts

You can connect Telegram to get a private message when something happens with your payments. This is optional.

## What you get alerts for

- When funds arrive at your deposit address.
- When the privacy step starts.
- When funds are ready to claim.

The alerts never include any private message content. They only tell you that a payment is waiting and to visit the claim page.

## How to set it up

### 1. Open your account area

Go to your dashboard or the claim page and find the Telegram option.

[SCREENSHOT: Telegram opt-in button in the account area]

### 2. Sign to confirm

Your wallet asks you to sign a short message. This links the alerts to your account. It is free and moves no funds.

### 3. Start the bot

You get a one-time link to the VeerTx Telegram bot. Open it and start the bot. The link is single use and expires after a short time.

[SCREENSHOT: Telegram bot start screen]

### 4. Done

You now get alerts in Telegram. You can do this from any wallet linked to your account.

## Privacy

- Alerts say a payment is waiting. They never reveal amounts or private messages.
- The link between your Telegram and your account is stored only to send you these alerts.
- You can stop alerts at any time by blocking the bot.
